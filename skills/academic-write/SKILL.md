# Academic Write

First identify the paper type and its central claim. A benchmark centers the
task, data, evaluation and findings; a method paper centers its mechanism and
evidence; an application paper centers deployed behavior and measured outcomes.
Keep that contribution hierarchy while editing.

Use the author's current materials as facts. Never invent experiments,
citations, experience or outcomes. State only what the evidence supports and
distinguish model-assisted judgments from independent human evaluation.
