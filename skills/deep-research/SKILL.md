# Deep Research

Produce an evidence-grounded investigation that answers explicit research
questions. Preserve the current installed survey workflow; the former
13-agent package is a historical archive, not an additional default workflow.

Start with the topic, two or three answerable research questions, angle and
reader. Clarify only information needed to choose the scope. A short lookup
does not need a full survey. Without retrieval capability, state the limitation
instead of producing a literature report from memory.

Each phase links its narrower protocols. Open them when that decision is
needed; do not concatenate all phases, examples or references at startup.
Advance to the next phase when the task reaches it. Retain sources and unresolved
questions across phases rather than rerunning completed searches.

Verify every cited source. Distinguish reported findings, inference, conflict
and unavailable evidence. Synthesize competing perspectives, answer the research
questions, and run the applicable quality gates before delivering a full report.
Use Chinese by default when the user asks in Chinese; preserve the user's
requested language and scope. Parallel search is optional and follows the
environment's delegation authorization.
